Mathematical Formulas
🎯 Sticky Aim Calculation
Stickiness = (100 − ADS_Sens) × AA_Multiplier × Device_Factor
Where AA_Multiplier = 1.4 (advanced) or 1.2 (standard); Device_Factor = 1.0 (phone) or 0.9 (tablet). Higher result = stickier aim assist.
⚡ Sensitivity Conversion
New_Sens = Base × (Screen_Diagonal / 6.5) × (DPI / 400)^0.5
Converts sensitivity between different screen sizes and DPI settings.
🎮 Gyroscope Scaling
Gyro_Scope = Base_Gyro × Zoom_Multiplier × Stability_Factor
Zoom multipliers — Red Dot 1.0x · 3x 0.7x · Sniper 0.5x · 4x 0.6x · 6x 0.4x
💀 Time To Kill (TTK)
TTK(ms) = (Shots_To_Kill − 1) / RPM × 60000 + ADS_Time
Where Shots_To_Kill = ceil(100 / Damage_Per_Shot)

🎯 Sticky Aim Mastery
What is Sticky Aim?
Sticky aim refers to how much the aim assist "sticks" to targets. Lower sensitivity means stronger stickiness because the game assists more when you move slower across a target.
🎮 Camera vs ADS Sensitivity
Camera (Steering): movement when NOT aiming — affects 180° turns and checking corners.

ADS Sensitivity: movement when AIMING — this is where stickiness matters most.

Rule: keep Camera higher than ADS for quick reactions, but ADS low for sticky aim.
🔴 Red Dot vs Sniper Scope
Red Dot (1x): use 1.2–1.4× your ADS sens — good for close-mid tracking.

3x Tactical: use 0.8–0.9× ADS sens — best AR range.

Sniper: use 0.5–0.7× ADS sens — maximum precision required.
🏆 Pro Settings Formula
For tournament-level sticky aim: Camera 90–110 · ADS 70–90 · Red Dot = ADS × 1.3 · 3x Scope = ADS × 0.9 · Sniper = ADS × 0.6 · Aim Assist ON + Advanced
